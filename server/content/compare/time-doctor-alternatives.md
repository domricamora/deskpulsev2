## Why teams leave Time Doctor

**It is the priciest option, and the gap widens with headcount.** At 20 seats the
difference against a $7 tool is around $94 a month. At 100 seats it is $470 a month —
$5,640 a year for the same job.

**There is no free tier.** Most of the category offers at least a single-user free plan
to evaluate on. Time Doctor does not, so evaluation means a trial clock and a purchase
decision.

**Workers push back on the interventions.** Idle prompts and distraction alerts are
effective for some teams and corrosive for others. If your workforce already resents
monitoring, features that interrupt them make the rollout harder, not easier.

**Per-seat pricing punishes the growth you are working for.** Every agent you hire raises
a fixed monthly cost permanently.

## Switching from Time Doctor

1. Export timesheets and reports as CSV before you cancel. Screenshot history does not
   migrate between vendors — no tool in this category imports another's.
2. Run both in parallel for one pay period. Idle thresholds and activity definitions
   differ between products; find the variance before it reaches a client invoice.
3. Tell your team first, and lead with what changes for them.
4. Rebuild any client-facing report before cutover.
5. Cancel after one clean reconciled cycle.
