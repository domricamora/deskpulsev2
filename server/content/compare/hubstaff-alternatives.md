## Why teams leave Hubstaff

Hubstaff is a genuinely capable product with a decade of engineering behind it. The three
reasons teams look elsewhere are consistent.

**1. The advertised price isn't the price.** Starter is $4.99/seat but has no screenshots
— the feature most teams are buying monitoring software for. Screenshots, timesheet
approvals and unlimited activity require the **{{vendor.hubstaff.tier}} tier at
{{vendor.hubstaff.price}}/seat**. The Insights add-on is another **$2.50/seat** on top. A
"$4.99" tool is realistically $12.50.

**2. Per-seat pricing punishes growth.** This is the complaint we hear most from
outsourcing firms. Every agent you hire raises your bill, permanently. Going from 20 to 40
seats doubles your monitoring cost in a year when your margins are already under pressure.

**3. Tracking is where it stops.** Hubstaff tracks time well and pays contractors, but
most teams still run a separate spreadsheet for local payroll and a separate tool for
client invoicing. Three systems, reconciled by hand.

## How to choose

Work out your seat count in 12 months, not today. Then price each option at that number
rather than at your current headcount — the whole difficulty with per-seat pricing is that
the decision looks cheap at signup and expensive at renewal.

Ask three questions:

1. **Does the tier I'm quoting actually include screenshots?** On Hubstaff it does not
   until {{vendor.hubstaff.price}}. This one question changes most comparisons.
2. **What happens to my bill when I double headcount?** Every tool here except DeskPulse
   answers "it doubles".
3. **How many systems am I still running afterwards?** If payroll and client invoicing
   stay in spreadsheets, the monitoring tool solved a third of the problem.

## FAQ

**What is the cheapest Hubstaff alternative?**

Time Champ at {{vendor.timechamp.price}}/seat has the lowest per-seat price of any serious
monitoring tool. At larger seat counts DeskPulse is cheaper in total because the bill caps
at {{price.cap}} — at 100 seats that is {{price.cap}} against $690.

**Does Hubstaff have a free plan?**

Hubstaff has a free tier limited to one user, and it does not include screenshots.
Clockify has the most generous free tier in the category, up to five users, but it is a
time tracker rather than a monitoring tool.

**Which Hubstaff alternative is best for a BPO?**

DeskPulse or Time Doctor. Both are built for client-billed outsourcing work. DeskPulse
adds payroll, per-client contract rates and the price cap; Time Doctor has the longer track
record and a bigger support organisation.

**Do I lose my data if I switch from Hubstaff?**

Export timesheets and reports as CSV before you cancel. Screenshot history does not
migrate between vendors — no tool in this category imports another's screenshots — so
retain anything you might need for a billing dispute before closing the account.
