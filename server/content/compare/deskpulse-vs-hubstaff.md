## Where Hubstaff genuinely wins

We'd rather you knew this now than after switching.

**Contractor payments.** Hubstaff pays your team directly from tracked hours via Wise,
PayPal or Payoneer. If that's how you pay 40 people every fortnight, it's a real workflow
and we don't replace it. You'd keep a separate payment rail.

**GPS and mobile.** If you have field staff, drivers or anyone working off a desktop,
Hubstaff tracks them and we don't. This isn't a gap we're planning to close — we're built
for desk-based outsourcing work.

**Integrations.** Asana, Jira, Trello, QuickBooks, Gusto and 25 more, natively. Our
integration story is thin. If your project-management tool must sync automatically, factor
in that cost.

**Maturity.** Hubstaff has been shipping for over a decade with a large support
organisation and a deep documentation library. We're newer. On a Friday night incident,
that difference is real.

## Where DeskPulse wins

**Your bill stops.** The single biggest structural difference. Above
{{seats.cap_engages}} seats, hiring costs you nothing more in monitoring. For a BPO
planning to grow from 40 to 90 seats, that is the difference between one line item and
one that doubles.

**The workflow runs further.** Hubstaff hands you tracked time and a CSV. DeskPulse takes
the same tracked time through payroll — overtime approvals, adjustments, payslips — and
out the other side into client invoices, with per-client contract rates and per-employee
bill rates held separately. That is a spreadsheet and a reconciliation job you stop doing.

**Effectiveness scoring is included.** Activity percentage against task completion,
ranked, built for performance reviews. On Hubstaff the comparable analytics live in the
Insights add-on at $2.50/seat — $162/month at {{seats.cap_engages}} seats, on top of the
subscription.

**Built for the BPO shape.** Per-client contracts *and* per-employee bill rates as
separate concepts, seven capability-based roles including a client/external viewer, and an
audit log as standard. These exist because outsourcing operations need them, not as
enterprise upsells.

**Consent-first as a design position.** Tracking runs only when the worker presses Start.
A persistent monitoring indicator is always visible. Screenshots are blurrable and never
appear on public client share links. Both products support disclosure; we have made it
structural rather than configurable, which is an easier conversation to have with a
workforce that is wary.

## Which should you choose?

**Choose Hubstaff if:** you pay contractors through the platform; you need GPS or mobile
tracking; a specific integration is load-bearing; you're under 10 seats and not growing;
you want the largest support organisation.

**Choose DeskPulse if:** you're a BPO, outsourcing firm or agency; you're above 20 seats
or heading there; you're reconciling payroll or client invoices in a spreadsheet today;
your bill going up every time you hire is a problem; you want monitoring your team won't
fight you on.

## Switching from Hubstaff

1. Export timesheets and reports as CSV before cancelling — screenshot history does not
   migrate between vendors.
2. Run both in parallel for one pay period. Idle thresholds differ between products;
   understand the variance before it reaches a client invoice.
3. Tell your team first, and lead with what changes for them.
4. Rebuild client-facing reports before cutover.
5. Cancel Hubstaff after one clean reconciled cycle.

Two weeks, mostly human rather than technical.
