## If you are an employee being monitored

Contact **your employer first** — under data-protection law they are the controller of
your monitoring data and only they can correct or erase it. If they do not respond, write
to privacy@deskpulse.click and we will identify the controller and route your request. The
[privacy policy](/privacy) sets out exactly what is and is not captured.

## Before you write about pricing

Most pricing questions are answered on the [pricing page](/pricing), and the
[cost calculator](/tools/cost-calculator) will give you your own number in about ten
seconds. If you need above [SEAT CAP] seats, custom terms, SSO or a signed DPA, mail
sales@ and say so directly — that is an Enterprise conversation and we will not pretend
otherwise.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Send it to security@deskpulse.click with enough detail to reproduce. We acknowledge within
3 business days and will keep you informed until it is resolved. We will not pursue legal
action against researchers acting in good faith who avoid privacy violations and give us
reasonable time to fix an issue before disclosure.
