## 3. Acceptable use — monitoring

This section is not boilerplate. It is the condition on which the service is offered.

**You must not deploy DeskPulse covertly.** You agree that:

- Every monitored person is **told, in advance and in writing**, that monitoring is in
  place, what is captured, and for what purpose.
- You will not attempt to conceal, disable or misrepresent the agent's monitoring
  indicator, or install the agent on a device without the knowledge of the person who uses
  it.
- You will not install DeskPulse on a personal device without the owner's informed
  agreement.
- You are solely responsible for complying with the employment, privacy, works-council
  and surveillance laws that apply where your workers are located, including completing
  any legitimate-interests assessment or obtaining any consent those laws require.
- You will not use DeskPulse to monitor anyone who is not a worker engaged by you — not
  family members, not partners, not minors.

We may suspend or terminate an account we reasonably believe is being used for covert or
unlawful surveillance, without refund.

## FAQ

**Can I use DeskPulse without telling my staff?**

No. Covert deployment is a breach of these terms and grounds for termination without
refund. It is also unlawful in much of the world.

**What counts as a billable seat?**

Any staff login in your workspace — employees, managers, HR and IT administrators —
excluding read-only client portal viewers. Your bill follows your live seat count.

**Do I need a credit card for the trial?**

No. Payment is by bank transfer and only when you decide to continue.

**Can I cancel any time?**

Yes. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you have paid for; there is no
annual lock-in on the monthly plans.

**What happens to my data if I leave?**

You have 30 days from termination to export it, after which it may be permanently deleted.
